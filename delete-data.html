This page explains how to delete the data held by the Find Me Stay Android app, published by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME]. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which describes what is collected and why.

The important thing to know first: we operate no accounts and no server of our own. There is nothing to sign out of and no profile held by us. Almost everything the app knows about you is stored on your phone, which means you can delete it yourself, immediately, without asking anyone.

How to request deletion

  1. Delete your saved details In the app, open Account → Remove my details → Remove. This erases your saved first name, last name, email address and phone number from the device straight away.
  2. Clear your search history Account → Clear recent searches removes every destination, date and guest count the app remembered.
  3. Uninstall the app to remove everything else Uninstalling deletes all remaining app data from your phone — saved hotels, your local booking list, cached prices, currency and theme preferences, and the stored region estimate. Nothing survives the uninstall.
  4. To delete booking records held by our supplier Email support@findmestay.com from the address used for the booking, with the booking reference and the words "data deletion request" in the subject. We acknowledge within 7 days and pass the request to Nuitée Travel, who hold the reservation. See the limits below.

What we cannot delete

We are a booking app, not the merchant of record. Once you complete a reservation, the record of it belongs to Nuitée Travel and to the hotel you booked. We can pass your deletion request to them, and we will, but we cannot delete a reservation record ourselves and neither can they always do so — a completed booking is a financial and tax record that businesses are usually required to keep.

The same applies to your card details, which are entered directly into the payment provider's own page and never pass through our app or reach us in any form.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, or to object to how it is processed. Because we keep no server-side copy of anything, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device using the steps above. For anything held by Nuitée Travel, write to us and we will forward the request.
